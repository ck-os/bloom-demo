    Oakleaf Hydrangea

    NC native

    Sun

    Part shade — tolerates more sun than other hydrangeas with consistent moisture

    Watering

    About 1 inch/week, more during hot dry stretches — mulch reduces how often this is needed.

    Pruning window

    Right after bloom finishes — blooms on old wood

    Wildlife value

    Blooms attract pollinators; exfoliating bark and fall color add winter interest

    Regenerative practices

    • Mulch to retain moisture for its relatively shallow roots
    • Minimal fertilizer needed once established
    • Blooms on old wood — prune only right after flowering, never in late winter/spring
